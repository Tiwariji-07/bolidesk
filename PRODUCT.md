# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian service-business owners and office staff who are often on a phone between jobs. They need to turn WhatsApp-style job notes into accurate money requests without switching between spreadsheets, chats, and calculators.

## Product Purpose

BoliDesk turns a spoken or typed job brief into a quote, invoice, payment request, and practical follow-up plan. Success means less admin at the end of a workday and clearer cash collection.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, reassuring. It should feel like a well-organised back office that understands Indian service work, not a finance dashboard built for accountants.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS marketing cards, crypto-like gradients, dense enterprise tables, and decorative dashboards that hide the next action.

## Design Principles

- Put the next cash-moving action in reach immediately.
- Let operational detail be compact but never cryptic.
- Make money, due dates, and state legible at a glance.
- Treat mobile use as normal, not a compromised desktop view.
- Earn trust through calm, direct language and visible calculations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard-accessible controls, clear focus states, labelled forms, semantic page structure, and colour-independent status cues. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
